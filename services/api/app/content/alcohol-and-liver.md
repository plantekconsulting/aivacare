## Why we recommend no alcohol
When the liver already has fat or inflammation, alcohol adds further injury and speeds up scarring. For people with MASLD, and especially with any fibrosis, the safest amount of alcohol is none.

## Tips for cutting out alcohol
Plan alcohol-free alternatives for social events, tell friends and family about your goal, and notice triggers such as stress or certain routines. If stopping is difficult, tell your care team; support is available and nothing to be ashamed of.
