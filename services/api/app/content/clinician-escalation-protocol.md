## Priority and response times
High priority: acknowledge within 24 hours; covers FIB-4 above 2.67, ALT above 3x ULN, and any patient-reported red-flag symptom (red flags also trigger immediate patient instructions). Medium priority: acknowledge within 72 hours; covers indeterminate FIB-4 and ALT doubling from baseline. Low priority: within 7 days; covers wearable trends such as weight gain, activity drop or resting heart rate rise, handled by the care coordinator.

## Re-escalation
If a task is not acknowledged within its SLA it is escalated to the backup clinician and the clinic lead.

## Standard follow-up actions
Common approved actions include ordering elastography, repeat liver panel, dietitian referral, intensified coaching cadence, and medication review at the next visit. AI-generated proposals are drafts; the responsible clinician must approve before any care-plan change is communicated to the patient.

## Dietitian referral criteria
Refer to the LiverWell dietitian when weight has risen more than 3% from the lowest recent value, when ALT rises alongside weight gain, or when the patient requests support.
