## FIB-4 bands
FIB-4 below 1.3: low risk of advanced fibrosis; repeat in 1-2 years (annually if diabetic). FIB-4 1.3-2.67: indeterminate; perform second-line assessment with vibration-controlled transient elastography (VCTE) or ELF test. FIB-4 above 2.67: high risk; arrange VCTE and hepatology review. Age over 65 reduces specificity; use 2.0 as the lower cutoff in that group.

## Elastography interpretation
Liver stiffness below 8 kPa: advanced fibrosis unlikely. 8-12 kPa: indeterminate, consider biopsy or MR elastography. Above 12 kPa: advanced fibrosis likely; assess for MASH-targeted therapy eligibility.

## Rising transaminases
ALT doubling from the patient's own baseline warrants review of alcohol use, new medications or supplements, weight trajectory and glycemic control, with repeat LFTs in 4-6 weeks. ALT above 3x ULN requires review within 24 hours and exclusion of other causes (viral, autoimmune, drug-induced).

## Therapy considerations
Resmetirom is indicated for non-cirrhotic MASH with moderate-to-advanced fibrosis (F2-F3) alongside lifestyle therapy. GLP-1 receptor agonists support weight and glycemic goals. All therapy changes require hepatologist decision.
