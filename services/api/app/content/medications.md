## Your current medicines
Keep taking your medicines as prescribed. Never stop or change a medicine without talking to your care team.

## Metformin
Metformin helps control blood sugar in type 2 diabetes. It is safe for people with fatty liver disease.

## Statins
Statins such as atorvastatin lower cholesterol and protect the heart. They are safe in fatty liver disease and should not be stopped because of mildly raised liver enzymes unless your doctor advises.

## Resmetirom
Resmetirom is a once-daily tablet approved for adults with MASH and moderate to advanced liver scarring, used together with diet and exercise. Your hepatologist decides whether it is right for you.

## GLP-1 medicines
Medicines such as semaglutide help with weight loss and blood sugar and can reduce liver fat. They are prescribed based on your overall health picture.

## Supplements
Some herbal and bodybuilding supplements can harm the liver. Check with your care team before starting any supplement.
