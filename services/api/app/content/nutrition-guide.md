## The Mediterranean-style plate
A Mediterranean-style eating pattern has the strongest evidence for fatty liver. Fill half your plate with vegetables, a quarter with lean protein (fish, poultry, beans, lentils, tofu) and a quarter with whole grains. Use extra-virgin olive oil as your main fat, and include a small handful of nuts most days.

## Foods to limit
Limit sugar-sweetened drinks, fruit juice, sweets and pastries, white bread and white rice, processed and red meats, and fried or ultra-processed foods. Fructose from sweetened drinks is especially linked to liver fat.

## When liver enzymes are rising
If your ALT is trending up, the most effective first steps are: cut out sugary drinks entirely, swap refined carbohydrates for whole grains and legumes, keep portions consistent, avoid late-night snacking, and avoid alcohol completely. Small, sustained changes work better than strict short-term diets.

## Coffee
Regular, unsweetened coffee (2-3 cups a day) is associated with less liver scarring. Skip the sugar and syrups.

## Practical swaps
Swap soda for sparkling water with lemon. Swap white rice for brown rice, quinoa or lentils. Swap chips for a handful of unsalted nuts. Swap a creamy dressing for olive oil and vinegar.
