## How much activity helps
Aim for at least 150 minutes of moderate activity per week, such as brisk walking, cycling or swimming. Exercise reduces liver fat even before you lose weight. A practical daily target is about 8,000 steps.

## Strength training
Add resistance exercise (bands, weights or bodyweight) two days a week. Building muscle improves how your body handles sugar, which helps the liver.

## Getting back on track after a slump
Activity often dips during busy or stressful periods. Restart with short 10-minute walks after meals, then build up by about 1,000 steps per week. Your wearable trend is a helpful guide; consistency matters more than intensity.

## Safety
Check with your care team before starting vigorous exercise if you have heart disease, chest pain, or feel unusually short of breath.
