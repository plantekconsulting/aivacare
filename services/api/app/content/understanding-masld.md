## What is MASLD?
Metabolic dysfunction-associated steatotic liver disease (MASLD), previously called non-alcoholic fatty liver disease (NAFLD), means extra fat has built up in your liver. It is closely linked to excess weight, type 2 diabetes, high cholesterol and high blood pressure. It is the most common liver condition worldwide and often causes no symptoms.

## What is MASH?
In some people the fat causes inflammation and liver cell injury. This is called metabolic dysfunction-associated steatohepatitis (MASH). Over years, MASH can cause scarring (fibrosis). Advanced scarring is called cirrhosis. The goal of your care plan is to stop and reverse this process early.

## Can it be reversed?
Yes. The liver can heal. Losing 5% of body weight reduces liver fat; losing 7-10% can reduce inflammation, and around 10% or more can improve scarring. Steady lifestyle changes sustained over months matter more than quick results.

## How we monitor your liver
Your care team tracks blood tests (ALT, AST, platelets), a calculated score called FIB-4, your weight, and activity from your wearable. Trends over time matter more than any single result.
