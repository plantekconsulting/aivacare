## Seek urgent care immediately
Call 911 or go to the nearest emergency department if you have: vomiting blood or material that looks like coffee grounds; black, tarry stools; new confusion, extreme drowsiness or difficulty staying awake; or severe abdominal pain.

## Contact the clinic the same day
Call LiverWell Clinic the same day if you notice yellowing of your skin or the whites of your eyes, new swelling of your belly or legs, or a fever with abdominal pain.

## Not sure?
If you are unsure whether a symptom is serious, call the clinic. In an emergency, do not wait for a message reply.
