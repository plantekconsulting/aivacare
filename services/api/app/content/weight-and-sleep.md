## Setting a realistic weight goal
A loss of 7-10% of your starting weight over 6-12 months is the target for most people with MASLD. Losing about 0.5-1 kg per week is safe. Very rapid weight loss is not recommended.

## When weight creeps back up
Regain is common and is not a failure. Re-check the basics: sugary drinks, portion sizes, evening snacking and activity. Weigh yourself at the same time each week and focus on the trend.

## Sleep matters
Short or poor-quality sleep is linked to weight gain, higher blood sugar and more liver fat. Aim for 7-8 hours. Keep a regular schedule and limit screens before bed. Loud snoring with daytime sleepiness can indicate sleep apnea, which is common in fatty liver disease; mention it to your care team.

## Resting heart rate
Your wearable's resting heart rate tends to fall as fitness improves. A sustained rise can reflect poor sleep, stress, illness or reduced activity.
