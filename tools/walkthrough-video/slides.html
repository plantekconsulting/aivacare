The problem

Today: appointment-based care

  • Labs, records and Apple Watch data in separate systems
  • Nobody watches the trend between visits
  • Concerning changes noticed late
  • Follow-ups coordinated by hand

Goal: continuous care

  • Monitor patients between visits
  • Personalized, clinician-approved guidance
  • Right findings to the care team, on time
  • Physicians stay in control
